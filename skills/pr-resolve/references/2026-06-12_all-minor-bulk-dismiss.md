- **Rule:** When every active finding is Minor with a plausible skip
  rationale, offer a single bulk-dismiss before the per-item triage loop.
- **Why:** Forcing a full per-item pass on all-Minor findings is friction the
  user routes around — they reach for "dismiss all and merge" anyway.
- **Where:** wk-pr-resolve → Step 4, "All-Minor bulk-dismiss gate" (before
  Order of processing).
