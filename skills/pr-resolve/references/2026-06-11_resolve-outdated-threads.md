- **Rule:** After confirming an outdated thread's concern is gone, call
  `resolveReviewThread` — do not merely note it as auto-skipped.
- **Why:** An unresolved outdated thread still counts toward the PR's
  open-thread total and reads as outstanding feedback to branch protection
  and reviewers.
- **Where:** "Filter active comments" section — the auto-skip instruction.
