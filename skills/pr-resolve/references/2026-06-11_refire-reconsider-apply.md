- **Rule:** On the re-fire gate, surface the re-fire count and offer an
  "(d) apply the bot's fix now" option; lean toward applying when the fix is
  clean/low-risk and the concern is structurally valid.
- **Why:** Dismiss-with-rationale silences one round, but bots re-evaluate
  from current source every push — repeated re-fires signal an unaddressed
  structural concern, not a stuck bot.
- **Where:** Step 4 — "detect bot re-review thrash" HARD RULE, 3rd-re-fire prompt.
