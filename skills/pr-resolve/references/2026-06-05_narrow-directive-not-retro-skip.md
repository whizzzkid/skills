- **Rule:** A narrow user directive ("fix the description, mark ready")
  defines what to do, not when to stop — Step 11 retro fires on every
  session completion regardless of instruction scope.
- **Why:** A scoped directive was treated as the full task, overriding
  the skill's mandatory closing retro step.
- **Where:** Step 11 (Session Retro) — directive-scope clause.
