# Derive blast radius from lens findings, not diff type

- **Rule:** The executive summary derives blast radius from the eight-lens
  findings (SPOFs, assumption failures, delivery risk); never pre-judge it from
  diff size or "doc-only."
- **Why:** Arch-review exists to determine blast radius. Starting from "it's
  doc-only so blast radius is low" inverts the reasoning and can suppress
  attention on a high-impact spec.
- **Where:** Step 4 REVIEW mode — Executive Summary.
