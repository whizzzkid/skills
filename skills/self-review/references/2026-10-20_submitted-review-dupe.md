**Rule** — Before re-staging a self-review after new commits, list your reviews and branch on state. A submitted
review's comments are already public: stage only new or changed notes and reply on stale threads.

**Why** — The update flow assumed the prior review was still pending. When it had been submitted there was nothing to
delete, and re-posting the full payload duplicated every comment in a new pending review.

**Where** — `SKILL.md` → Updating an Existing Self-Review, step 0.
