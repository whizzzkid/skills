- **Rule** — Full descriptive names apply in every scope (test code, one-off
  locals, inline temporaries); initialisms and ad-hoc aliases are always wrong
  unless they are the established project convention.
- **Why** — The "descriptive names" rule was silently relaxed for test-local
  variables (`ciCases` for case-insensitive cases); the rule has no scope
  exemption.
- **Where** — Rules section, sub-bullets under "Descriptive variable names".
- **Source** — distilled from learnings/skills/workstyle-naming/2026-06-11_no-initialism-variable-names.
