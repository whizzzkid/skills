- **Rule:** A post-interview scorecard is always a booked 30–45-min calendar
  event, never a checkbox or to-do item in any caller's dashboard.
- **Why:** When surfaced as a checkbox, the user has to schedule it themselves
  and it gets dropped; <30 min is also too short to finish a scorecard.
- **Where:** §Interview Prep Scan, Step 2B (HARD RULE + 30–45-min window).
