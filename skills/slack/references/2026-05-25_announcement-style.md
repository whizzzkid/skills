- **Heading pattern:** `:topical-emoji: *Subject — Context subtitle*` — em-dash separates noun from context; emoji chosen for the subject, not decoration.
- **tl;dr:** One or two conversational sentences explaining why the message exists ("We've been heads-down..."), not corporate ("This document summarizes...").
- **Numbered lists** for ordered or sequential items (epics, stack PRs, rollout steps); **bullets** for unordered (pending reviews, links, options).
- **Nested sub-bullets** use `    ◦ ` (4-space indent + ◦) for stacked PR chains or multi-part items.
- **CTA is an invitation:** "Would love eyes on sequencing and the open questions" — not a demand with a deadline.
- **Warm close only when ending week or going OOO:** "Have a good weekend folks" / "I'll be away Monday".
- **Direct asks:** "Hey Folks, one-line ask. :emoji: <url|label>" — no preamble, no signature.
- **Never over-tag:** CC only who genuinely needs the ping. One or two `<@handle>` is normal.
- **No sign-off block:** No "Thanks, Nishant" — the Slack profile is the signature.
