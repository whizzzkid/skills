**Rule:** Before executing the first deliverable of a multi-sentence prompt,
enumerate every deliverable and commit to the full list. A message opening with
a noun task and closing with an imperative is two work items, not one task plus
elaboration — never stop after the first.

**Why:** A re-violation of the existing Continuity Rule. The rule was present
verbatim but did not steer: the agent completed the opening task and stopped,
requiring an explicit "why did you stop?" prompt. Escalated one notch (baseline
→ `**Important:**`) per the re-violation ladder.

**Where:** Continuity Rules, first bullet.
