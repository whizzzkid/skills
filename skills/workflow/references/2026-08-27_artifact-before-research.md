## Principle

A prompt or task referencing a concrete artifact (URL, review comment, PR,
issue) → fetch and exhaust that artifact before dispatching parallel research
agents.

## Why

Parallel investigation without the linked artifact's content wastes cycles on
misdirected research. The artifact often contains the answer or narrows the
research scope significantly.

## Rule landed

Autonomy Rules → "Linked artifact first" bullet.
