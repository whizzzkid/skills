**Rule:** When a correction or mistake surfaces mid-run, invoke `wk-learn` in
the same turn — offering to capture ("want me to capture it?") is still asking,
which the mandatory-capture rule forbids.

**Why:** Post-completion learning capture is mandatory, not opt-in. A
concise-mode reflex to end a turn with a short offer collides with the standing
rule, converting a required action into a permission request and forcing the
user to re-prompt.

**Where:** wk-workflow §Learning capture HARD RULE (re-violation escalation of
the existing "never ask whether to capture learnings" rule).
