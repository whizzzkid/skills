- **Rule:** Never open with "good catch" / "great point" / "nice find" —
  agree ("you're right", "correct") or just state the fix.
- **Why:** Grading the reader's observation positions the agent above them and
  reads as patronizing.
- **Where:** wk-tone → Banned register (patronizing-praise bullet) + Step 3
  pre-send grep.
