# Feedback about a person reads to its subject

- **Rule:** For feedback written about a person, open with one genuine line about working with them, bold the one or
  two takeaways per section, and close with a warm sign-off. Keep fact-check tables and verification notes out of the
  paste-ready text; report them in chat.
- **Why:** A drafted peer-review reply opened with a bare acknowledgement, ended on a correction line, and kept a
  fact-check table in the same file. The user rewrote it with a personal opener praising the working relationship and
  the person's candid feedback, bolded takeaways, and a sign-off, and removed the table and the correction. The draft
  had optimized for factual review, not for a recipient reading feedback about themselves.
