- **Rule:** In the contradiction audit, check the current run's own edits and
  recent `.distilled-sources.log` entries; when a newer/higher-severity
  learning reverses a recent rule, replace it and record `Supersedes:` in the
  new reference — never leave both rules live.
- **Why:** The freshest rules are the most likely to be reversed, since
  format/approach churn arrives as a cohort of same-day learnings; the audit
  otherwise only scans for old stale prose and stacks contradictory rules.
- **Where:** Step 5 "Contradictory rules" bullets.
