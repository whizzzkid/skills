**Rule** — Measure the staged SKILL.md body with the hook's `measure()` BEFORE
drafting any content-adding fold. Unconditional — not gated on "headroom looks
tight."

**Why** — The over-ceiling / at-ceiling state is invisible until measured. Drafting
first, then discovering the ceiling at commit, forces a reactive measure-and-trim
loop (the re-violation signal) instead of one planned decisive cut.

**Where** — Step 7.5 de-bloat gate, "Budget the reclaim before drafting" bullet.
