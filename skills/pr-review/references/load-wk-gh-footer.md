**Rule**

Invoke `wk-gh` (Skill tool) before drafting any review body or inline comment, to
extract the canonical outbound footer. "Do not invent one" means load `wk-gh` — not
skip the footer. Omitting the footer is a rule violation, not an acceptable fallback.

"don't invent" as permission to omit produced review bodies and inline comments with no
footer at all.
