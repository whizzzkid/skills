Never use "good catch" (or "Good catch") in replies, comments, or messages.

**What happened:** Agent opened a reply to a reviewer comment with "Good catch —" before stating the fix.

**Root cause:** Default praise-then-explain pattern treats reviewer observations as needing affirmation. "Good catch" reads as patronizing — it positions the agent above the reviewer, grading their observation.

**Suggested fix:** Ban the phrase outright. Alternatives: "You're right —", "Correct —", "Valid —", or just state the fix directly without praising the observation. The fix speaks for itself.
