Peer-feedback replies need a warm relationship opener, bolded takeaways and a sign-off; keep the file paste-ready.

**What happened:** The drafted peer-review reply opened with a bare "happy to! here's my take", ended with a
correction line, and kept a fact-check table in the same file. The user rewrote it: added a personal opener praising
the working relationship and the person's candid feedback, bolded the key takeaway sentences, added "All the best!",
and removed the fact-check table and the closing correction line.

**Root cause:** The draft optimized for factual review rather than for the recipient reading feedback about
themselves.

**Suggested fix:** For feedback written about a person: open with one genuine line about working with them, bold the
one or two takeaways per section, close with a warm sign-off, and keep verification notes out of the paste-ready text
(report them in chat instead).
