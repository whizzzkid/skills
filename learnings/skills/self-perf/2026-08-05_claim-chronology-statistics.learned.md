Performance narratives must verify chronology and statistical interpretation before converting evidence into impact
claims.

**What happened:** A narrative called one milestone the first preview despite earlier evidence and treated a
non-significant duration result as proof that durations did not change.

**Root cause:** The synthesis compressed multiple source milestones and converted failure to establish significance
into evidence of absence.

**Suggested fix:** During synthesis, enumerate dated milestones before using first or before language, distinguish
execution units with similar names, and phrase non-significant results as insufficient evidence rather than no effect.
