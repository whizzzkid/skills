"Make it crisper" means trim wording inside the existing structure, not collapse sections.

**What happened:** Asked to make a long draft less wordy, the agent cut it by ~60% and merged sections and bullets. The
user interrupted and asked to undo it; they preferred the previous format.

**Root cause:** Interpreted a tone/wording request as a length/structure request without confirming the target.

**Suggested fix:** For "crisper/less wordy", keep every section, bullet and fact; cut filler and duplicate clauses
(~15–20%). Confirm before any cut larger than that or any structural change.
