Self-reflection drafts must be action-first; never frame growth as directed by others.

**What happened:** The draft paraphrased past review themes as "I was asked/told to…", implying a manager directed the
work. The user corrected it twice: no one did the asking, and the whole reflection should read as what they did.

**Root cause:** Feedback themes from prior reviews were rendered in passive, attributed voice instead of as
self-directed habits.

**Suggested fix:** In performance/self-reflection writing, use active declarative verbs ("I set", "I led", "I
shipped"); state misses as learning with the correction already in motion; ban "I was asked/told", "I tried",
"helped with".
