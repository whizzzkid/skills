## Session-2

### What worked
- Resolve a multi-file add/add merge conflict by verifying the incoming side is a strict superset (`git show <ref>:<path>` diff) before `git checkout --theirs`, rather than re-deriving content by hand.
- When a pending self-review blocks REST inline replies (HTTP 422), route around it via a single consolidated conversation comment plus GraphQL `resolveReviewThread` — never submit the author's held review.
- Triage a chained bot finding as scope-creep when the proper fix requires introducing new tooling inconsistently (one-off DSL RBI); revert cleanly and defer with a documented rationale rather than force a disproportionate fix under a Minor severity.

### What could've been better
- wk-commit: the amend prohibition holds regardless of push state — an unpushed commit is not a license to self-initiate `git commit --amend`.
