## Session-6

### What worked
- Resolving an env-var naming conflict (requested name vs. an external plugin's actual contract) with a single AskUserQuestion, then conforming to the tool's contract, avoided a silent guess.
- Reusing an existing external-plugin feature (its built-in multi-credential collection) instead of hand-rolling parallel rotation logic honored the codebase's fleet-first reuse mandate.

### What could've been better
- wk-pr: when a change relies on an external tool/plugin supporting a feature, cite the verifiable source (link the specific source file/line proving it) in the PR body and self-review up front, rather than only proving it after a reviewer challenges whether the capability was hallucinated.
