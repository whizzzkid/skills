## Session-6

### What worked
- Re-listing the comment surface immediately after every conversation write caught a silent overwrite before the user or the bot's owners could notice; verify-after-write on external mutations is worth enforcing as a rule.
- Capturing another author's comment body before writing near it made an accidental overwrite fully recoverable.

### What could've been better
- gh: POST to the single-comment resource updates the comment instead of erroring; a "reply" on the conversation surface must always create a new comment on the issue-comments collection (routed to wk-learn, captured live).
