## Session-1

### What worked
- Distinguishing infra-side vs. code-side CI failures by reading log position (before vs. after a step's command runs) correctly avoided a red-herring root cause.
- Folding an automated review finding (mis-stated comment rationale) into a targeted follow-up commit, with a reply acknowledging the fix, kept review lineage clean without a full re-review cycle.

### What could've been better
- wk-buildkite: when reporting a post-merge build failure, state separately whether the merge *triggered* the build run versus whether the diff *caused* the failure inside it — collapsing both into one "unrelated" verdict drew a correction from the user.
