## Session-3

### What worked
- Post-rebase spec validation caught abstract-class breakage (test helpers instantiating a now-abstract class) before pushing — the validation step prevented a broken push.
- Agent-based reconciliation for a complex stacked-PR rebase (30+ add/add conflicts from duplicated history) produced a correct result verified by 96 passing specs.

### What could've been better
- [pr-update]: When a stacked PR locally duplicates parent-branch commits that main merged independently, the merge strategy produces massive add/add conflicts. Detect the "independently-merged parents" pattern (compare branch's pre-fork commits against base's merged PRs) and suggest `--onto` rebase or cherry-pick of only the branch's own commits, skipping the duplicated history.
