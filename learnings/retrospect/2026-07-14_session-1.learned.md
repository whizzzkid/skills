## Session-1

### What worked
- **Backward-compat via stable prefix:** When evolving an embedded identity/signal marker, use a stable *prefix* constant (not literal-string swaps). Every existing `include?` matcher then recognizes both new and legacy forms without updating.
- **Proactive coverage closure:** Pin dedup/integration tests to the NEW emitted format before adversarial review, not just to legacy literals — prevents a blocker on the very test the change affects.
