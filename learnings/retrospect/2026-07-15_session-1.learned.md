## Session-1

### What worked
- Two-tier artifact split (fully-scrubbed published doc + unscrubbed in-repo grounding notes marked internal-only) cleanly satisfies "ground in real sources but scrub before publishing."
- Grep sweep for a known internal-term denylist plus an independent fresh-subagent leaked-identifier check gives higher confidence than either scrub pass alone.
- When an external data source is unreachable, leave a TODO naming the exact metric/query to pull once authorized rather than fabricating figures or blocking the whole deliverable.

### What could've been better
- wk-commit: user-requested in-repo reference/grounding docs were left uncommitted as "ephemeral scratch," forcing the user to ask twice to include them — stage explicitly-requested in-repo artifacts by default.
