## Session-1

### What worked
- Baseline every red gate against the default branch's lockfile before attributing failures to a dependency bump.
- A fresh adversarial subagent reading upstream gem source catches transitive-major regressions that a green suite misses.

### What could've been better
- renovate: treat any transitive major bump as a mandatory changelog/behavior audit before claiming the combined PR is safe.
- self-review: before re-staging after new commits, check whether the prior review was already submitted and post only delta notes.
