## Session-1

### What worked
- A clean-content PR revival (base merged+deleted) reduced to a mechanical `git rebase --onto origin/<default> <last-base-commit> HEAD` + new PR — zero conflicts because the rebased commits were disjoint from the squashed base.
- Reused the prior adversarial-review clearance on identical content: recorded a fresh clearance for the rebased HEAD, treated review suggestions as pre-existing follow-ups rather than expanding the revive's scope.

### What could've been better
- wk-pr-takeover: no documented path for reviving a PR auto-closed by base-branch merge+deletion; discovered reopen and base-retarget both fail on a closed PR only after trying them.
