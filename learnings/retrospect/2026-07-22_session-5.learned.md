## Session-5

### What worked
- Stacked producer/consumer work across two repos with a full URL cross-reference in the PR Meta section keeps the dependency legible to reviewers.
- Single-sourcing two related payload keys from one call (never computed twice) removes a class of "the two fields disagree" bugs.

### What could've been better
- wk-gh: the CI-green rollup check must count an entry non-terminal when EITHER `.status` (CheckRun) OR `.state` (commit Status) is pending/in-progress — a predicate reading only `.statusCheckRollup[].status` silently treats a PENDING commit Status as green and reports settled early.
