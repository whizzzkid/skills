## Session-16

### What worked
- `git rebase --onto origin/main <parent-of-first-new-commit>` precisely isolates a feature's commits when a PR was mistakenly stacked on a now-merged branch; one conflict, cleanly resolved.
- Adversarial subagent caught a signal-safety regression and a byte-wise UTF-8 truncation bug that both compiled and passed tests; two-pass review (full branch + blocker re-review) confirmed clean before push.

### What could've been better
- pr-resolve: rebase conflict resolution dropped a safety guard (`signal.Stop`) that existed on `origin/main` but not in the incoming commit; the skill's conflict-resolution step should audit whether the "losing" side contained signal/context/cleanup primitives not present in the merged result.
