## Session-N

### What worked
- Merging a stack bottom-up and moving each ticket to Done on merge kept the epic's state accurate throughout a 6-PR train.
- A real code fix that addresses a bot's blocking finding flips an auto-approve-on-fix bot to APPROVED with no manual gate action.

### What could've been better
- pr-resolve: a bot review holding a PR at REVIEW_REQUIRED must trigger reading the bot's finding body (including summary-comment "findings outside diff") and fixing it, before any gate-level escalation (wait/dismiss/override).
