## Session-N

### What worked
- The adversarial-review gate caught a binding brand/design-system violation (semantic status colors used decoratively) before merge; declining the cosmetic request with written rationale beat shipping a rule-breaking change.
- On a merge-only ruleset that requires an up-to-date head, merging the base into the branch (clean, no conflict) and re-pushing is the reliable path to a CLEAN state before merge.

### What could've been better
- wk-workstyle: before implementing a cosmetic color/border change requested by a bug report, check the repo's binding brand/design rules (AGENTS.md, design docs) first — a decorative use of the semantic status palette is a Phase-2 violation, not something to surface only at adversarial review.
