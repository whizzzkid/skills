## What worked

- Revalidated the remote head, required checks, approvals, review threads, child relationships, and active merge policy immediately before mutation.
- Verified the merged state, merge commit, and remote branch deletion instead of treating a successful command exit as completion.

## What could've been better

- `[wk-pr-merge]`: Avoid a redundant branch-deletion question when the invoked workflow already defines deletion as its merge-command default; expose an explicit keep-branch option instead.
