## Session-4

### What worked
- Shipping a feature as a stack of small, independently-green PRs, each cleared by a fresh adversarial-review subagent before the next, kept every diff reviewable.
- Mutation testing (invert the logic under test, confirm the targeted spec fails, revert) proved new specs were not tautological before trusting them.
- Cross-referencing local rev-parse, ls-remote, and the PR head-oid resolved "which SHA is really the branch head" after a remote-side change.

### What could've been better
- git: a plain-grep empty result was treated as proof of absence; use git grep for tracked-content verification.
- git: after a --update-refs rebase, verify the checked-out branch before committing — a commit landed on the wrong stack branch.
- pr-update: no playbook for a base moving under a stacked set; always rebase the whole stack onto the new base when a parent merges or auto-update-branch injects a merge.
- self-review: a pending review is orphaned by a force-push and must be deleted and re-posted against the new HEAD.
- commit: expect the auto-mode classifier to block a force-push after a rebase and surface the command for approval rather than stopping.
