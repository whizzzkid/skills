### What worked
- Adversarial review caught a tautological regression test (asserting behavior already guaranteed by an orthogonal validation path); re-target such tests at the truly-unguarded field.
- Refusing to bypass commit signing with `--no-gpg-sign` on a transient agent failure, surfacing the blocker instead — the failure self-resolved on retry.

### What could've been better
- pr-resolve: after resolving all comments on a draft PR, mark it ready with `gh pr ready` without being asked.
- pr-merge: on a merge signal, enter the skill directly rather than hand-doing a cosmetic pre-step first.
- pr-merge: recognize repeated push-triggered bot re-evaluation ("bot-thrash") surfacing an unbounded tail of new Minors, and stop the push cycle rather than chasing each one.
