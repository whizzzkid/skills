## Session-3

### What worked
- Jira lifecycle after `gh pr ready`: update stale ticket-description notes, transition to In Review, and post a footer-carrying PR-opened comment in one pass — keeps the ticket a faithful mirror of the shipped PR.
- Resolving an external tool's opaque 404 by fetching the canonical resource list first, rather than retrying a guessed identifier.

### What could've been better
- jira: MCP calls need the tenant UUID cloudId (resolved via getAccessibleAtlassianResources), never the site hostname — a hostname 404s and a guessed hostname compounds it.
