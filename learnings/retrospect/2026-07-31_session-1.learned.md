## Session-1

### What worked

- Resolve review fixes from the bottom of a stack upward, propagate each verified parent tip, and
  use tree comparison to distinguish content changes from history-only rewrites.
- Verify every merged member and the default branch against the atomic merge commit before
  starting post-merge cleanup.

### What could've been better

- wk-adversarial-review: Resolve an explicit review-tool opt-out before probing or dispatching that
  tool, and treat the waiver as final; routed to `wk-learn adversarial-review`.
- wk-pr-merge: Require parity between local and submitted stack membership before gating or
  merging a subset; routed to `wk-learn pr-merge`.
- wk-git: Probe signing configuration from the exact launch context before committing in a
  temporary worktree; routed to `wk-learn git`.
