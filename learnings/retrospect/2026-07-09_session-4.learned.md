## Session-4

### What worked
- Scoping the adversarial review to the parent-cleared merge-base delta (not the default-branch diff) avoids re-reviewing already-cleared stacked parents.
- Verifying an external dependency's exception hierarchy by reading the installed gem source, rather than assuming, correctly disposed of a "rescue too narrow" finding without over-fixing.

### What could've been better
- workstyle-ruby: an RSpec controller spec that stubs a shared base-controller filter needs a concrete named subclass, not an anonymous `controller do` block — the block form trips Sorbet/BlockMethodDefinition vs the no-metaprogramming cop.
- workstyle-ruby: prefer a plain sig'd class/module with an inlined filter over an ActiveSupport::Concern when the app has no concern precedent and Sorbet requires_ancestor + `included do` fails.
- workstyle-ruby: a regex requiring a literal single space should use `\x20` not `[ ]` (Style/RedundantRegexpCharacterClass) and not a bare space (invisible/error-prone).
- adversarial-review: an auth-scheme parser matching a fixed-case literal and a single-space separator is not RFC 7235 conformant — flag case-insensitive scheme and 1*SP separator on any Bearer/HTTP-auth header parse.
