## Session-1

### What worked
- Scoped re-review (diff since last cleared SHA, not full branch) kept adversarial review fast and focused after incremental fixes.
- Bot findings triaged one-at-a-time held the consultation loop clean; prior-round dismissals surfaced automatically.
- Adversarial subagent caught a missing "Read tool" instruction in the classifier prompt — the inline diff → file-path refactor silently broke the model's ability to access the diff.

### What could've been better
- wk-pr-resolve: no guidance for context-compaction resume — when a session restarts mid-skill, the first action should be identifying the last completed step and resuming from there, not restarting from the top.
- wk-workflow: announcing a skill invocation in text ("running adversarial review now") without actually invoking it in the same turn creates a silent gap the user must notice and correct.
