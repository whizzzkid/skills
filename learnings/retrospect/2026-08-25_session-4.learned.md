## Session-4

### What worked
- Tracing a CI failure to its actual root cause (cross-spec Redis leak via a missing after-hook) by diffing against a passing base-branch build, rather than assuming the diff itself was the cause, correctly identified a pre-existing bug.
- Squash-merging behind a full pre-merge checklist (CI, reviews, thread severity triage, action-item scan) caught and fixed three real gaps (unchecked test-plan items, stale scope claim) before merge.

### What could've been better
- wk-buildkite: after pushing a CI fix, don't claim the next build "should pass" -- fetch and report the actual build/job state before making any pass/fail claim.
- wk-pr-resolve: a Minor-severity bot finding with a concrete, cheap fix (add a couple of unit tests) was deferred to a post-merge "file a ticket" offer instead of being fixed in the resolve pass; severity label alone shouldn't override an available obvious-fix classification when the user explicitly asked to resolve comments now.
